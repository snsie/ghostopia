# Characters (task2, vertical slice)

Every character is generated by `blender/scripts/build_characters.py` (see README "Rebuild") and exported to
`assets/characters/<name>.gltf` with its animations. Machine-readable data (sizes, bones, nodes, clips,
loop flags, no-slide speeds, item clips, events) is in `assets/characters/characters.json`. Scale: 1 block =
1 m. Pivot = centre of the base on the ground; front = Godot +Z. 24 fps, no root motion.

Sources: `concept_art/basic ghosts.png` and `concept_art/basic ghost.png` (player ghost, which override
everything else: **no mouth**), `concept/characters/villagers.png`, `scare_reactions.png`,
`worker_ghosts.png`, `spectral_tools.png`, `concept/locations/cottage_interior_night.png`.

## Player: Basic Ghost (`ghost`)

| | |
|---|---|
| Height | 0.60 m sheet (0.64 m with the hem points); hovers by moving the pivot (1 block up = hem at 1.0 m, eyes at 1.44 m) |
| Silhouette | rounded dome head flaring into a draped bed sheet, six soft folds, pointed wavy hem that turns inward (dark inside), no legs |
| Materials | `GHOST_sheet` glossy white, alpha 0.86, pale ice-blue emission; `GHOST_manifest` (opaque, brighter) on the hidden `MATERIAL_manifest` node; fresnel rim = Godot shader |
| Eyes | `ghost_eyes`, two dark violet-black ovals, shape keys `angry`, `scared` (wide), `happy` (squint), `closed`; calm = basis. No mouth |
| Arms | sheet "mitten" arms inside the body; `upperarm_*` scale 0.04 = hidden, 1 = out (tools, BOO) |
| Rig | 13 bones: `root body chest head`, `upperarm/forearm/hand_L/R`, hem bones `hem_F hem_BL hem_BR` |
| Held | `HELD_tool` in the right palm, fits the task1 tool `GRIP` (left hand IKs to `GRIP_off` in the tool clips) |

Clips: `idle_float` (loop: bob, hem ripple, arms hidden), `drift` (loop: forward lean, trailing hem),
`glide_down` (loop: hem flares up), `ascend` (loop: stretch, hem trails down), `phase` (squash, stretch
forward, eyes shut), `swing_scythe`, `dig_shovel`, `smash_hammer` (two-handed, tier I tools), `haunt_cast`
(reach into an object), `boo` (rise, arms out, eyes wide), `hurt`, `scatter` (burst and shrink to nothing,
Godot adds the wisps), `celebrate` (spin, arms up, happy eyes).

## Villagers (shared skeleton, 21 bones)

`root hips spine chest neck head hat shoulder_L/R upperarm_L/R forearm_L/R hand_L/R thigh_L/R shin_L/R foot_L/R`.
Bodies are lofts, clothes are shells, skinning by code (≤ 3 bones per vertex). Face mesh `<name>_face`
with shape keys `uneasy scared terrified relieved asleep` (calm = basis): big dark oval eyes with a
highlight, brows, rosy cheeks, mouth. Hats are a separate mesh on the `hat` bone. `HELD_R` / `HELD_L` in
the palms. Every villager wears a folklore charm on the belt.

| `villager_maid` | 1.60 m (bonnet) | slim, nervous; white bonnet, wavy auburn hair, puffed sleeves, lilac skirt, apron with a back bow | cream, dress_lilac/purple, apron_white, hair_brown | lantern (`HELD_R`, hanging) | salt pouch on a strap + herb sprig |

Shared clips (every villager has all 20, baked for its own proportions): `idle`, `walk`, `walk_uneasy`
(faster, hunched, glancing left then right), `look_over_shoulder`, `call_out` (raise the held light,
other hand cupped at the mouth), `clutch_candle_walk`, `startle` (small hop), `big_scare_leap` (hat flies
off, lantern dropped, lands crouched), `flee` (run, arms flailing), `laugh_it_off` (rubs the back of the
head, chuckle bounce), `suspicious_search` (loop: light raised, scanning), `hang_charm`, `scatter_salt`
(three throws from a pouch), `relight_candle`, `sit`, `rock_in_chair`, `sleep` (lying on the back, eyes
`asleep`), and the work loops `sweep`, `work_field`, `serve_drinks`.

Notes for Godot:
- Expressions are keyed inside the clips (uneasy while glancing, terrified in the leap and flee,
  relieved when laughing). Godot can also set the shape keys directly.
- `item_clips` in `characters.json` lists the clips that show the default item; hide the `HELD_*`
  children in the others (or attach the task1 `broom` for `sweep`, a tray for `serve_drinks`).
- `big_scare_leap` events: hat off at frame 8, item dropped at frame 11, landing at frame 28.
- `sit` / `rock_in_chair`: pivot on the seat surface (`MARK_sit`); feet reach 0.48 m down (the child's
  dangle). `sleep`: pivot on the mattress (`MARK_sleep`), head toward the facing direction.

## No-slide speeds (step 1 / step 5)

The clips have no root motion. Godot moves the villager and scales the clip's playback speed by
`actual_speed / no_slide_speed`. At the no-slide speed a planted foot moves backward at exactly the body's
speed (measured: error ≤ 0.5 mm/s over every planted frame of every gait clip). Speeds are in m/s, with
the loop length in frames at 24 fps.

| Villager | Leg (m) | `walk` | `walk_uneasy` | `clutch_candle_walk` | `flee` (run) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `villager_widow` | 0.542 | 0.56 (24 f) | 0.699 (36 f) | 0.702 (34 f) | 1.976 (14 f) |
| `villager_farmer` | 0.642 | 0.663 (24 f) | 0.827 (36 f) | 0.831 (34 f) | 2.339 (14 f) |
| `villager_maid` | 0.622 | 0.643 (24 f) | 0.802 (36 f) | 0.805 (34 f) | 2.266 (14 f) |
| `villager_teacher` | 0.702 | 0.725 (24 f) | 0.905 (36 f) | 0.908 (34 f) | 2.556 (14 f) |
| `villager_child` | 0.363 | 0.474 (19 f) | 0.602 (28 f) | 0.615 (26 f) | 1.685 (11 f) |

`walk_uneasy` and `clutch_candle_walk` hold two gait cycles per loop (the glance goes left, then right).

Ghosts have no feet, so any speed works. The clips are tuned for these speeds:
- Player: `drift` 2.5–4 m/s, `idle_float` below 0.5 m/s, `glide_down` / `ascend` for vertical speed
  beyond ±0.5 m/s.
- Workers: `carry` at about 1.5 m/s.
